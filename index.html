About IICT Unofficial

Welcome to our Website

This website has been specially created to support and guide juniors of the institute. Our main goal is to make student life easier by providing useful information, connections, and updates in one place.
✨

This platform is also designed to help students easily remember and recognize their college seniors, making interaction and bonding much easier.

The purpose of this platform is: To help juniors with important academic and college-related information To make it easy for students to remember and connect with their seniors To build a helpful and friendly student community

This website will be managed and controlled by Saurabh Raj for the next 4 years. After that, it will be passed on as a gift to the juniors, who will continue to manage and improve it.

🚀 More features and updates will be added in the future to make this platform even better.
